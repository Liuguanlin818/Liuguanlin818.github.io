    About Me

    I hold a B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Shandong Normal University, where I graduated with a GPA of 3.94/5.0 (89.4/100), in the top 6% of my cohort. I have honed my proficiency in programming languages, including C, C++, Python, Java, and so on. Throughout my undergraduate studies, I won the school scholarship and the honorary title of outstanding student every year. Besides, I actively participated in a variety of national academic competitions, earning over ten awards, including those from the National College Student Mathematical Modeling Competition and the Blue Bridge Cup Programming Competition.

    In addition, I have been deeply involved in several research projects spanning AI applications in healthcare, large language models for rumor detection, embedded systems, and humanoid robotics. Among them, I successfully led the application and completion of five National/Provincial College Student Innovation Training Projects and other funded research initiatives. These experiences resulted in multiple research outputs, including publications in Expert Systems with Applications (SCI Q1), Knowledge-Based Systems (SCI Q1), Information Processing and Management (SCI Q1), and the KDD-AIDSH 2024 Conference. I have also been granted two patents and two software copyrights.
