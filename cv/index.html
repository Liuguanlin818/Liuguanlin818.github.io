    Publications

    ENCMed: A 3D-Encoder Framework with Neighbor Contrastive Learning for Safe Medication Recommendation first author
    G. Liu, Z. Liu, X. Yu, X. Li, X. Zheng, X. Fan
    Manuscript under review, 2026.
    A three-branch encoder that fuses molecular fingerprints with drug–drug interaction graphs and a patient’s visit sequence, trained with neighbour contrastive learning so that the recommended combination is both accurate and safe.
    CLEAR: Prototype-conditioned flow purification for LLM-based rumor detection with Dirichlet evidential learning
    Z. Liu, H. Wu, X. Fang, G. Liu, et al.
    Information Processing & Management IF 8.1, 2026. [DOI]
    Conditions a flow-matching generative model on prototype representations and adds Dirichlet evidential learning, so that an LLM-based detector returns calibrated uncertainty instead of a bare label.
    DNMDR: Dynamic Networks and Multi-view Drug Representations for Safe Medication Recommendation first author
    G. Liu, Z. Liu, X. Yu, X. Li, X. Zheng, X. Fan
    Knowledge-Based Systems IF 8.0, 2025. [DOI]
    Rebuilds a patient’s history as a weighted dynamic network whose edges are inferred from conditional probabilities, and represents drugs from several views at once so that drug–drug interactions are penalised during combination prediction.
    Transformer-based medication recommendation with a multiple graph augmentation strategy co-author
    X. Li, X. Yu, G. Liu, X. Fan, F. Ge, Y. Zhao, X. Zheng
    Expert Systems with Applications IF 8.2, 2024. [DOI]
    Applies a Transformer over augmented drug and diagnosis graphs to capture higher-order relations between medical codes before predicting the medication combination.
    DG-MRM: Dynamic Graph and Multi-view Medication Information for Recommending Medication Combination first author
    G. Liu, Z. Liu, X. Yu, X. Li, X. Zheng, X. Fan
    KDD-AIDSH 2024 — AI and Data Science for Healthcare workshop, 2024. [OpenReview]
    The workshop version of the dynamic-graph medication recommendation line, reporting the first results on MIMIC-III.
