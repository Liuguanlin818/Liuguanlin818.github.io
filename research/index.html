    Research Experience

    I have worked on machine learning and software projects across several kinds of data. They cover electronic health records, language models, edge computing and robotics. All of them were carried out at Shandong Normal University, most within an undergraduate research programme.

    Explainable deep learning methods for electronic health records05/2023 – 08/2026
    Supervisor: Prof. Xiaomei Yu, Shandong Normal University

    Constructed a dynamic graph neural network over multi-source electronic health record data and applied a deep-learning based recommendation algorithm to suggest accurate and safe drug lists to clinicians, towards personalised and precise medication recommendation. Outputs include a registered software copyright for the medication recommendation system (V1.0, 2024-03-29).

    Fake news detection based on large language models02/2023 – 05/2026
    Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Hongchen Wu, Shandong Normal University

    Analysed the semantic interaction between posts and comments in social-media datasets to explore how ambiguous event descriptions influence emotional responses to real and fake news, and proposed and implemented detection algorithms. The project won the National Second Prize at the 16th China University Computer Design Competition.

    Intelligent classroom management system based on edge computing05/2022 – 05/2023
    Supervisor: Dr. Ke Liu, Shandong Normal University

    Built a sound-source localisation and tracking device on a Raspberry Pi 4B with infrared laser ranging, and a contactless temperature measurement, mask detection and face recognition system. Won the Second Prize at the 2022 Shandong Province College Student Electronic Design Competition and the First Prize at Shandong Normal University’s Electronic Design Competition; patent granted as first inventor (2022-10-19, No. 202222749613.8).

    Human–robot interaction methods for NAO robots07/2021 – 06/2022
    Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Wei Yan, Shandong Normal University

    Developed an intelligent obstacle-avoidance system for NAO robots using computer vision, and a music-domain human–robot interaction system combining natural language processing with knowledge-graph methods. Won the Third Prize at the 24th China Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Competition (Shandong); software copyright for the intelligent robot obstacle avoidance system V1.0 and patent granted as first inventor (2023-10-24, No. ZL 2023 2 0465706.X).
