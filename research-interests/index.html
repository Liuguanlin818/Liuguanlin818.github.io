    Research Interests

    Artificial intelligence for healthcare

    I am interested in applying machine learning to medicine and health, using convolutional, graph neural networks as well as large language models, on both biomedical signals and clinical records. On the application side, I am interested in biomedical signals, especially EEG data and brain-computer interfaces for applications such as emotion recognition and seizure detection. I am also interested in clinical data, such as electronic health records, for clinical prediction and medication recommendation.

    Large language models and intelligent agents

    I am interested in large language models and in intelligent agents. On the language side, I am interested in generative applications such as question answering, summarisation and information extraction. I am also interested in retrieval-augmented generation, where a model draws on external sources. On the agent side, I am interested in systems that plan, use tools, and keep memory across a long task. I am also interested in systems made up of several cooperating agents.
